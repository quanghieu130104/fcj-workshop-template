### Purpose of the Event

* Share experiences about job hunting after graduation and effective communication skills.
* Provide practical knowledge, skills, and real-world experience in Cloud Computing.

### List of Speakers

I do not remember all of the speakers' names. I only remember that there were four speakers who shared their experiences and knowledge throughout the event.

### Key Highlights

* The impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on the software development industry and how AI is gradually changing the role of developers.
* Introduction to Cloud Computing and its key components, including cloud services, scalability, security, and deployment models.
* Career orientation and practical advice for students preparing to enter the IT industry.
* Real-world experiences and best practices from industry professionals.

### My Experience at the Event

Participating in the Cloud Computing event was a valuable experience that gave me a broader understanding of modern cloud technologies and the digital transformation taking place in today's software industry. The event also helped me better understand current technology trends and the skills required for future careers.

#### Learning from Industry Experts

The speakers shared valuable insights from their professional experiences, including career development, communication skills, and practical knowledge about Cloud Computing. Their presentations helped me better understand what companies expect from new graduates.

#### Hands-on Technical Experience

Although the event mainly focused on presentations and discussions, it introduced practical cloud concepts, real-world use cases, and modern development practices used in the IT industry.

#### Exploring Modern Technologies

The event introduced several modern technologies, especially Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Cloud Computing. I learned how AI is transforming software development and how cloud platforms support scalable, reliable, and efficient applications.

#### Networking and Communication

The event provided an opportunity to interact with lecturers, speakers, and fellow students. Listening to different perspectives helped me gain more confidence in preparing for my future career.

#### Lessons Learned

From this event, I learned that technical knowledge alone is not enough for a successful career. Communication skills, continuous learning, adaptability, and keeping up with new technologies such as AI and Cloud Computing are equally important. The event motivated me to continue improving both my technical and soft skills.
